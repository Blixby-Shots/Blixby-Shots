Behind the Lens

I'm an automotive photographer and filmmaker based in Los Gatos, shooting across the Bay Area. What started as chasing cars with a camera turned into a full-time obsession with light, motion, and the details that make every build unique.

Whether it's a track-day Porsche, a widebody Supra at golden hour, or a brand shoot that needs to feel cinematic, I treat every car like it deserves its own cover story. Five years in, I've worked with private owners, brands, and motorsport teams — and I'm just getting started.
